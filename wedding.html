Dress Code & Notes

Dress code: Formal. We’d prefer no white, ivory or navy blue, please!

Heels: No stilettos are allowed. This is a strict policy due to the flooring in the Grade I listed building. Block heels are welcome. Anyone wearing stilettos will be made to wear ugly flip-flops or slippers from the second-hand box!

Drinks: As a heads-up, there will be no red wine served.

Parking: On-site parking is available free for all attending guests, and can be left overnight if needed.
