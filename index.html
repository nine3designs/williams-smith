The Venue

Wentworth Woodhouse

Where We’re Getting Married

We’ll say “I do” in the Long Gallery at Wentworth Woodhouse — a magnificent stately home just outside Rotherham, South Yorkshire. The full address is on your invite; for sat-nav, search for Wentworth Woodhouse, Wentworth, Rotherham.

Parking: there is free on-site parking for all attending guests, and cars can be left overnight if needed.

Visit the Wentworth Woodhouse website →

Taxis + Travel

Getting To & From The Day

By Car

Set your sat-nav for Wentworth Woodhouse — there is plenty of free parking on site, and cars can be left overnight if you’d rather not drive home.

By Train

The nearest stations are Rotherham Central and Meadowhall, both a short taxi ride from the house.

Taxis

We’d recommend pre-booking a taxi for the evening — the celebrations finish at 10:30pm. We’ll share our recommended local firms here closer to the day.
