Guest Information

Dress Code & Venue Guidelines

Dress Code

Formal Attire. We kindly request that guests avoid wearing White, Ivory, or Navy Blue.

Footwear Notice

Wentworth Woodhouse is a Grade I listed historic building. Stiletto heels are strictly prohibited to protect the floors (yes, gentlemen, that applies to you too!). Block heels, wedges, or flat shoes are encouraged.

Venue Beverage Policy

Due to strict protection policies for historic Grade I listed buildings, no red wine is served or allowed anywhere on site.

Parking & Overnight Stays

Complimentary on-site parking is available for all guests. Vehicles may be left overnight and collected the following morning.
